## What this measures, and what it does not

The gold set is **synthetic**: 43 hand-written KYC-style sentences with known values, 16 hard negatives (circular dates, table figures, public phone numbers, job titles) and 2 items of public contact details. Spellings are awkward on purpose (grouped phone numbers, lower-case PAN, no honorific before a name). It tests the rules on that kind of text. It is **not** a sample of real customer documents, so it cannot say how many real names or numbers the stage misses in the wild.
